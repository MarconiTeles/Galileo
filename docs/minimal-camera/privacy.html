Minimal Cam Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Minimal Cam is a professional camera application for iPhone. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, including its optional retouch feature powered by Snap Camera Kit.

Camera, microphone, and Photos

Minimal Cam requests access to the camera and microphone so you can preview and record video. It requests permission to add completed recordings to your Photos library. Your photos and videos remain on your device unless you choose to share or transfer them using features provided by iOS.

Camera Kit retouch

When you enable retouch on the front camera, Snap Camera Kit processes camera frames to locate facial features and apply the selected Lens. Minimal Cam does not create a Snapchat account, access your Snapchat profile, reconstruct a friend graph, or use Login Kit, Creative Kit, Bitmoji, Story Studio, Lens Cloud, or location-based Lenses.

Snap Camera Kit may collect anonymized product-interaction, crash, and performance information that is not linked to your identity. Snap and its affiliates may process that information according to the Snap Privacy Policy and Snap Terms of Service.

Information Minimal Cam collects

Minimal Cam does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your camera frames, recordings, name, email address, Snapchat profile, contacts, or precise location. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Retention and control

Recordings saved to Photos remain under your control and can be deleted using the Photos app. Minimal Cam does not retain a server-side copy. Information processed by Snap Camera Kit is governed by Snap's policies and applicable Camera Kit terms.

Children

Minimal Cam is not directed to children under 13 and its Camera Kit integration is not intended to serve users in the United States who are under 13 years old.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its integrations change. The revision date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
